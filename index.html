How this calendar works

Every morning, The Local National checks the public event calendars of 23 D.C. venues, universities, think tanks, civic groups and newsrooms. It adds in-person and hybrid conferences, summits and public forums in the District and nearby. Our editors check the biggest events against the organizers' own pages each week.

Listings come from organizers and can change. Confirm details with the organizer before you go.
